# Grafiti Drone

**A spray-can trigger attachment for the DJI Flip, built for artists who paint walls they have permission to paint.**

> Status: pre-MVP. Nothing here is a finished product. Do not fly a paint can near people, property you don't own, or a wall you don't have written permission to paint.

---

## Manifesto

**1. Walls belong to someone.** Every wall has an owner. We build for murals that are commissioned, permitted or offered. Painting without consent is vandalism, whatever tool you use, and this tool doesn't change that.

**2. Reach is a creative medium.** Muralists are limited by scaffolding, lifts, budgets and insurance. A small drone that can put a mark ten metres up opens new compositions, new scales and new ways for emerging artists to get seen.

**3. Build in the open.** Mount designs, firmware, test logs and failures are published here. If it broke, you'll read about it. If it worked, you can reproduce it.

**4. Safety is a feature.** Aerosols are flammable and drones fall. We weigh every gram, test tethered before we fly free, and publish what we find. We won't ship a feature until we can explain how it fails.

**5. The law comes first.** Aviation rules, hazardous-materials rules, property law and local permits come before the product. Every kit ships with a legal guide, a wall-permission template and a pre-flight checklist. Read [RESPONSIBLE_USE.md](RESPONSIBLE_USE.md) before you fly.

**6. Artists first.** We judge this project by whether working artists get more visibility and new techniques out of it. Novelty alone doesn't count.

## What this is

| Layer | MVP (now) | Product (later) |
|---|---|---|
| Can mount + trigger | Open-source 3D-printable designs, 2–3 tested variants | Our own mount, injection-moulded or SLS, quick-release |
| Actuator | Off-the-shelf micro servo | Matched, weight-optimised actuator |
| RF trigger | Bench-wired dev boards + big clunky button | 3D-printed handheld remote with embedded electronics |
| Drone | Stock DJI Flip, unmodified airframe | Same: no firmware or airframe hacks |
